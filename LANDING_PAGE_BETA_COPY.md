## Beta section

### Section heading
Built for route discovery. Still in beta.

### Copy
Route Hacker is a beta product focused on helping travellers find realistic flight combinations and understand route viability.

It is useful for:
- exploring combinations that are not obvious in standard search tools
- checking schedule-based routing options
- understanding risk, layovers, and transfer timing
- seeing pricing signals where live fare coverage is incomplete

It is not yet a full live-price flight search engine, and some routes may be partially priced, estimated, or require manual verification before booking.

### Key points
- Beta status is visible throughout the product
- pricing may be partial or estimated
- route coverage varies by market and date
- some combinations are best treated as planning guidance

### Small footer line
Route details, pricing signals, and availability are best treated as planning guidance. Final flight price and availability can differ.
