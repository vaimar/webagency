## Decisions since last stand-up
- The slice is frontend-only. No backend endpoint; `POST /api/trips/total` is deferred to F7 until a server-persisted trip exists.
- New `tripTotal.ts` + `TripTotalCard` on `/spots/:slug`. The Trip Ledger isn't reused (no floor basis, hand-typed amounts). TripGuide `PackageSummary` isn't reused (unrouted, invents prices).
- The stay line is always an estimate, with a dates note (`/api/hotels/curated` is undated: one night, six weeks out) and a group-size note (per-room rates).
- Missing, non-EUR or manual-check lines are shown but not summed. The headline then reads `from`. No placeholder amounts anywhere.
- `doorToTripPrice` is never used in the all-in.
- Out of band: `IslandHopService`'s hardcoded nightly rates (part of F6) have already been replaced in another session. Stops without a live quote are now flagged `MANUAL_CHECK_REQUIRED` and the total is marked partial.
