## 3. Backend work (slumber)

**`SpotPoiService` cache bug — fixed, with tests.** `/api/spots/pois` returned
results on a cache miss and `[]` on **every hit for 7 days**: `cacheResult` stored
`writeValueAsString(pois)` (a JSON array of DTOs) while `parsePoisFromJson` read it
back through `parsePoisFromNode`, which iterates `root.path("elements")` — a
`MissingNode` on an array. Measured 312 pois cold, 0 warm.

Fix: cache the raw Overpass envelope; `EMPTY_RESULT_TTL_HOURS = 1` for empty
results (was 7 days); `CACHE_CATEGORY` bumped to `spot_pois_v2` so stale-format
rows are never read. `SpotPoiServiceTest` asserts on the **second** call —
reverting the fix makes 4 of 7 tests fail. Full backend suite: 225 tests green.

Spec written up in `docs/SPOT-POI-CACHE-BUG.md`, including two secondary issues
left alone deliberately (Overpass failures return `200 []`; `radiusM` is accepted
by no controller).

## 4. The nav decision, and why it changed

Nav was cut 7 → 2 (Home + Hack flights) on a "what don't competitors do" argument.
The user then said *"I liked my map of wakeboard spots"*, and that reasoning was
wrong: flight search competes with Skyscanner on a thin, rented 13-day data
source, while a European cable-park map with real multimodal access competes with
nothing and the data compounds.

**Nav is now Home · Spots · Hack flights.** Spots is the front door, hack-flights
is the engine that prices the way in. The activity picker on `/spots` was reduced
to Wakeboard only — Surf and Scuba had always returned zero spots, Ski returned
one. Everything else stays reachable from the footer.

### The rule that did most of the work (the user's, not mine)

**A park selling an individual hourly tariff has a full-size cable.** A full-size
loop circulates several riders at once so an hour of cable time is an hour of
your time; a two-tower bi-poulie carries one rider at a time and is therefore
sold in fixed 12–20 minute sessions.

Tested across the whole catalogue, with **no counterexamples**:

| rig | sells hourly | session-only |
|---|---|---|
| FULL_CABLE | 21 | 6 |
| MIXED | 9 | 2 |
| **SYSTEM_2_0** | **0** | **9** |

Caveats that matter:
- **Group/whole-cable hourly hire does not count** (Delta 90€/h, Urban 90€/h for
  2–5 people, IWP 95€ for 3–4). Those are not individual hourly rates.
- The **inverse** is weaker: "no hourly price recorded" often just means the price
  wasn't found. It only implies a 2.0 when the *complete* tariff has been seen and
  contains no individual hourly option (that is how Poule was classified, as
  `INFERRED` with the reasoning written into `setup_notes`).

### What automated extraction was and wasn't good for

Three passes over 95 fetched sites. **Good at sorting** (56 likely parks / 6 boat
clubs / 4 shops / 13 unknown / 5 unreadable in ten minutes). **Bad at extraction** —
regex "cable counts" were phone numbers and postcodes; of 89 sites exactly one
produced a machine-verifiable spec. Price yield topped out at **14 of 42 tarif
pages (~⅓)** and a second pass gained one park while creating three false
negatives.

Two bugs worth remembering: prices must be anchored **forward** from the duration
token (nearest-match grabbed subscription cards — Amiens came out at 315€), and a
`\b` after `€` never matches (non-word char followed by space), which silently
suppressed most matches.

**Reading tarif images works where text extraction cannot.** 16 sites publish
prices only as JPEG/PDF; reading them directly got LakeCity and Fun Parc Brumath,
and correctly rejected Delta's sheet as being for Watergames rather than the cable.

**Every automated price that survived filtering was later confirmed by the
operator's own page: Amiens 25/48, Atlantic 23/59, Cascade 27/35, Étoile 25,
Brumath 24/52, Léry-Poses 24. Six for six.**

## 7. House rules established this session

- Write only what the operator states. `STATED` for their own words, `LIKELY` for
  a review or a strong signal, `INFERRED` for a rule-based deduction with the
  reasoning recorded in `setup_notes`. Confidence vocabulary is
  `INFERRED|LIKELY|STATED|CONFIRMED` (a check constraint enforces it).
- **Only ever write `true` for amenities.** Not finding "bar" on a site is not
  evidence a park lacks one.
- Never force a price into the wrong column. A 15-minute session is not an hour.
- Record operator inconsistencies rather than silently arbitrating them (Atlantic
  750 m vs 770 m; Cergy 820 m vs 800 m; Rouffiac Angoisse vs Lanouaille; Verberie
  board included vs 9€).
- No bulk extraction from aggregate databases (wakesquare) — EU sui generis
  database right. Individual operators' own factual pages are fine.
